# Delivery Contract

- Choose one available issue and create one atomic claim before mutation.
- Own one issue at a time.
- Read the product contract and issue dependencies before implementation.
- Branch from current `origin/main`.
- Keep the branch current and resolve conflicts affecting its PR.
- Open a focused PR that closes the owned issue.
- Do not merge until required observable checks are green.
- Confirm the issue is closed and exact `main` CI is green before claiming more.
- Do not inspect or communicate with the comparison repository.
- A closed queue or self-report is not external acceptance.

Architecture and implementation decisions belong to the team. The referee will
not decompose product work or provide solution advice.
